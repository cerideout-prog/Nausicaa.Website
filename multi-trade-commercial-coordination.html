Multi-Trade Commercial Coordination.

Step up to larger packages without the administrative risk.

Why this work gets left on the table

Local single-trade businesses often pass on lucrative multi-trade packages because managing subcontractors, back-to-back payment claims, and principal reporting is too risky or administratively heavy. You have the operational capability, but you cannot justify a full-time, in-house commercial team to handle the resulting paperwork.

What we bring

Nausicaa Consulting steps in as your project-specific commercial manager. We provide the commercial scaffolding that allows you to safely act as a head contractor, coordinate complex works, and protect your margins without expanding your permanent overhead.

What we manage

  • Subcontractor administration. Drafting and managing back-to-back agreements with your lower-tier trades to ensure your risks are passed down appropriately.
  • Payment claim rolling. Assessing subcontractor claims and cleanly rolling them up into your primary, compliant claim to the principal.
  • Risk & reporting. Managing variations, extensions of time, and strict principal reporting requirements on your behalf.
  • Commercial protection. Ensuring rigorous compliance with WA Security of Payment laws across all tiers so you don't get caught holding the bill.

Take on the larger build. Hand off the trade administration and commercial management to an experienced local professional.

What it costs

Priced around the project, not a standard rate card — the right fee depends on the number of subcontract tiers, the value of the works and how long the project runs. Ask and you'll get a figure before you commit to anything.

Something you should know

Nausicaa also works for contractors from outside the region — possibly the same ones you're coordinating trades for or against. We say so openly. We will not act for two businesses on opposite sides of the same job, and we will not use anything you tell us to help someone else, during the engagement or after it. We check before taking on new work and we write that check down.
