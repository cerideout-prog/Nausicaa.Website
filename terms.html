Terms of engagement.

This document is a structural draft. Clauses marked [AWAITING ADVICE] have deliberately not been drafted, because drafting them without legal advice creates more exposure than leaving them blank.

Why some clauses are blank

Nausicaa's sell-side clients are small businesses under the Australian Consumer Law definition, and terms issued on a take-it-or-leave-it basis are standard form contracts. Since 9 November 2023, unfair contract terms in such contracts are unlawful rather than merely void, and penalties apply to proposing or relying on one — not only to enforcing it. Liability caps, unilateral variation rights, one-sided termination and one-way indemnities are the terms most likely to be caught. They are not drafted here.

1. Engagement

1.1 These terms apply to all services provided by Nausicaa Consulting unless varied by a written engagement letter, which prevails to the extent of any inconsistency.

1.2 An engagement commences when the client accepts a written proposal or engagement letter, and is subject to the conflict check at clause 2 having been completed.

1.3 The scope of services is as set out in the engagement letter. Work outside that scope is quoted separately and does not commence until the client agrees in writing.

2. Conflict management

2.1 Nausicaa's Conflict Management Framework forms part of these terms and is incorporated by reference. A summary is published at /conflict-policy and the full framework is provided on request.

2.2 A conflict check is performed and recorded before every engagement. Nausicaa may decline an engagement on the basis of that check without disclosing which other client gave rise to the conflict.

2.3 Market concentration and standing consent

The Client acknowledges that Nausicaa operates in a geographically concentrated market in which a limited number of contractors, principals and suppliers are active, and that Nausicaa's value to the Client derives in part from its engagement across that market. The Client consents to Nausicaa providing services to other participants in that market, including parties with whom the Client competes or contracts, subject to the Conflict Management Framework, which forms part of this agreement. This consent does not extend to any matter falling within the Tier 1 prohibitions, in respect of which Nausicaa will decline or withdraw regardless of consent.

[AWAITING ADVICE] — whether this clause is effective, and whether it survives the unfair contract terms regime as against small business clients. It must not be buried in fine print and must not be drafted so broadly as to purport to consent to a Tier 1 conflict.

3. Withdrawal

3.1 Where a conflict arises that cannot be managed, Nausicaa will notify all affected clients in writing promptly on becoming aware of it.

3.2 Nausicaa will withdraw from the later-commenced engagement, except where withdrawal from a live Superintendent's Representative appointment would prejudice the administration of a contract or the position of parties relying on impartial certification, in which case Nausicaa continues that appointment and withdraws from the other engagement.

3.3 On withdrawal under this clause, fees are payable for work performed to that date only. No termination penalty applies to the client. Nausicaa will provide a reasonable handover.

3.4 Confidentiality obligations survive withdrawal indefinitely.

4. Confidentiality

4.1 Confidential Information means rates, margins, pricing methodology, tender strategy, commercial positions, claim strategy, and any information not in the public domain obtained through an engagement.

4.2 Nausicaa will not use Confidential Information obtained through any engagement for the benefit of any other client, or for its own benefit, at any time, including after that engagement ends.

4.3 Client information is held in separate, access-controlled storage. Nausicaa does not maintain shared working files across clients.

5. Capability Register

The Capability Register, and all data, verification records and methodology within it, remain the exclusive property of Nausicaa Consulting. Deliverables may incorporate extracts. The Client is granted a non-exclusive licence to use those extracts for the project for which they were provided. No licence is granted to reproduce, distribute or use the Register or extracts for any other project or purpose.

5.1 No client Confidential Information is reproduced into the Capability Register. Rate history in the Register is sourced from Nausicaa's own market work, published data, or with written permission.

6. Superintendent's Representative appointments

Where Nausicaa is appointed as Superintendent or Superintendent's Representative, it acts impartially in the assessment, valuation and certification of claims, variations and extensions of time as required by the Contract, notwithstanding that its fees are paid by the Principal. The Client acknowledges that Nausicaa will not accept direction as to the outcome of any certification, valuation or determination.

[AWAITING ADVICE] — whether this wording is consistent with AS4000 and current authority, and what liability attaches on certification.

7. Time-critical obligations

The Client acknowledges that statutory and contractual timeframes — including payment schedule timeframes under the Building and Construction Industry (Security of Payment) Act 2021 (WA) — are strict and generally not extendable. Nausicaa's ability to meet those timeframes depends on the Client providing complete instructions and documentation within the times notified. Nausicaa is not liable for consequences arising from the Client's failure to do so, provided Nausicaa has given reasonable notice of what is required and by when.

[AWAITING ADVICE] — whether this exclusion is effective, or whether statutory liability overrides it.

8. Scope boundary

8.1 Nausicaa does not provide legal advice. Where a matter requires legal advice, Nausicaa will notify the client, and the client is responsible for obtaining it.

8.2 Nausicaa does not provide financial product advice, tax advice or audit services.

9. Fees and payment

9.1 Fees are as set out in the engagement letter. GST applies to all fees.

9.2 Client travel and accommodation is recovered as a disbursement at cost.

9.3 Nausicaa accepts no commission, referral fee, rebate or other benefit from any party it recommends, introduces or assesses. All Nausicaa income derives from disclosed client fees.

[AWAITING ADVICE] — the tender support hours cap, the minimum commitment on award, the buy-out mechanism and the tail clause. Each is drafted in the framework document but none is reproduced here, because all four are unfair-contract-terms sensitive as against small business clients and require review before being offered.

10. Contingent fee disclosure

Where fees are included in the Client's tender price and payable contingent on contract award, the Client acknowledges that Nausicaa has a financial interest in the success of that tender. Nausicaa will disclose this arrangement to any third party where required by that party's procurement conditions, or where Nausicaa reasonably considers disclosure necessary.
