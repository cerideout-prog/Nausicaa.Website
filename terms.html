Terms of engagement.

These terms apply to all services provided by Nausicaa Consulting, unless varied by a written engagement letter. The version in force at the date an engagement is signed is the version that applies to it.

1. Engagement

1.1 These terms apply to all services provided by Nausicaa Consulting unless varied by a written engagement letter, which prevails to the extent of any inconsistency.

1.2 An engagement commences when the client accepts a written proposal or engagement letter, or when Nausicaa commences the services with the client's agreement.

1.3 The scope of services is as set out in the engagement letter. Work outside the agreed scope will be quoted separately. If the Client requests out-of-scope work and Nausicaa commences that work with the Client's verbal or written agreement, such work will be charged at Nausicaa's standard hourly rates unless otherwise agreed.

2. Conflict management

2.1 Nausicaa's Conflict Management Framework forms part of these terms and is incorporated by reference. A summary is published at /conflict-policy and the full framework is provided on request.

2.2 Nausicaa performs conflict checks prior to engagement. Nausicaa reserves the right to decline an engagement on the basis of a conflict check without disclosing which other client gave rise to the conflict.

2.3 The Client acknowledges that Nausicaa operates in a geographically concentrated market in which a limited number of contractors, principals and suppliers are active, and that Nausicaa's value to the Client derives in part from its engagement across that market. The Client consents to Nausicaa providing services to other participants in that market, including parties with whom the Client competes or contracts, subject to the Conflict Management Framework, which forms part of this agreement. This consent does not extend to any matter falling within the Tier 1 prohibitions, in respect of which Nausicaa will decline or withdraw regardless of consent.

3. Withdrawal

3.1 Where a conflict arises that cannot be managed, Nausicaa will notify all affected clients as soon as reasonably practicable.

3.2 Nausicaa will withdraw from the later-commenced engagement, except where withdrawal from a live Superintendent's Representative appointment would prejudice the administration of a contract or the position of parties relying on impartial certification, in which case Nausicaa continues that appointment and withdraws from the other engagement.

3.3 On withdrawal under this clause, fees are payable for work performed up to the date of withdrawal. Nausicaa will provide a standard handover of current working files. Any additional transition assistance requested by the Client will be charged at Nausicaa's standard hourly rates.

3.4 Confidentiality obligations survive withdrawal indefinitely.

4. Confidentiality

4.1 Confidential Information means rates, margins, pricing methodology, tender strategy, commercial positions, claim strategy, and any information not in the public domain obtained through an engagement.

4.2 Nausicaa will not use Confidential Information obtained through any engagement for the benefit of any other client, or for its own benefit, at any time, including after that engagement ends.

4.3 Nausicaa maintains appropriate access controls and information barriers to ensure Client Confidential Information is protected and is not accessible to unauthorised personnel or other clients.

5. Capability Register

5.1 The Capability Register, and all data, verification records and methodology within it, remain the exclusive property of Nausicaa Consulting. Deliverables may incorporate extracts. The Client is granted a non-exclusive licence to use those extracts for the project for which they were provided. No licence is granted to reproduce, distribute or use the Register or extracts for any other project or purpose.

5.2 No client Confidential Information is reproduced into the Capability Register. Rate history in the Register is sourced from Nausicaa's own market work, published data, or is recorded in a strictly aggregated and anonymised format that cannot be traced to the Client or Project.

6. Superintendent's Representative appointments

6.1 Where Nausicaa is appointed as Superintendent or Superintendent's Representative, it acts impartially in the assessment, valuation and certification of claims, variations and extensions of time as required by the Contract, notwithstanding that its fees are paid by the Principal. The Client acknowledges that Nausicaa will not accept direction as to the outcome of any certification, valuation or determination.

7. Time-critical obligations

7.1 The Client acknowledges that statutory and contractual timeframes — including under the Building and Construction Industry (Security of Payment) Act 2021 (WA) — are strict. The Client is solely responsible for tracking and meeting its own deadlines. Nausicaa will use reasonable endeavours to support these deadlines, provided the Client supplies complete instructions and documentation in a timely manner. Nausicaa accepts no liability for missed deadlines resulting from delayed instructions.

8. Scope boundary

8.1 Nausicaa does not provide legal advice. The Client is solely responsible for determining when a matter requires legal advice and for obtaining it.

8.2 Nausicaa does not provide financial product advice, tax advice or audit services.

9. Fees and payment

9.1 Fees are as set out in the engagement letter. GST applies to all fees.

9.2 Client travel and accommodation is recovered as a disbursement at cost.

9.3 Nausicaa accepts no commission, referral fee, rebate or other benefit from any party it recommends, introduces or assesses. All Nausicaa income derives from disclosed client fees.

9.4 Where Nausicaa provides tender support services on an hourly or daily rate, a maximum fee cap will be agreed upon in the engagement letter. Nausicaa will notify the Client when 80% of this cap is reached. Work beyond the cap will only proceed, and be invoiced, upon mutual written agreement.

9.5 If a contingent fee arrangement applies and the Client is awarded the contract, a minimum commitment of 150 hours per month for project delivery support applies for the first 2 months, as detailed in the engagement letter.

9.6 If the Client wishes to engage a Nausicaa consultant directly as an employee within 6 months of the end of an engagement, a placement fee of 15% of the consultant's starting annualised salary is payable.

10. Contingent fee disclosure

10.1 Where fees are included in the Client's tender price and payable contingent on contract award, the Client acknowledges that Nausicaa has a financial interest in the success of that tender. Nausicaa will disclose this arrangement to any third party where required by that party's procurement conditions, or where Nausicaa reasonably considers disclosure necessary.

11. Insurance

11.1 Insurance is arranged on a per-engagement basis, at the types and limits required by the Client and recorded in the engagement letter. Cover arranged for an engagement may include:

(a) Professional Indemnity insurance; and
(b) Public Liability insurance.

11.2 A certificate of currency for the insurance arranged for an engagement will be provided upon the Client's written request.

12. Limitation of liability

12.1 To the maximum extent permitted by law, the total aggregate liability of either party to the other arising out of or in connection with this agreement (whether in contract, tort including negligence, statute, or otherwise) is limited to the amount recoverable under the relevant party's professional indemnity or public liability insurance policy, up to a maximum limited by insurance policy.

12.2 Neither party will be liable to the other for any indirect, consequential, or special loss, including loss of profit, loss of revenue, or loss of business opportunity.

12.3 This limitation of liability does not apply to:

(a) liability arising from fraud, willful misconduct, or criminal acts;
(b) breaches of confidentiality (Clause 4); or
(c) the Client's obligation to pay fees for services rendered.

13. Termination

13.1 Either party may terminate an engagement at any time, for any reason, by giving the other party 14 days' written notice.

13.2 Either party may terminate an engagement immediately by written notice if the other party:

(a) commits a material breach of these terms and fails to remedy that breach within 14 days of receiving written notice to do so; or
(b) becomes insolvent, enters administration, or is otherwise unable to pay its debts as they fall due.

13.3 Upon termination, the Client must pay Nausicaa for all services performed and reasonable expenses incurred up to the effective date of termination. Nausicaa will promptly hand over all Client data and relevant work-in-progress to facilitate a smooth transition.

14. Governing law

14.1 These terms are governed by the laws of Western Australia, and the parties submit to the non-exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of that State.

Version history

These terms are amended from time to time. The version in force at the date an engagement is signed continues to apply to that engagement. Superseded versions are retained and are available on request.
