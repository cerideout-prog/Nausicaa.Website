How we work

Engagement models.

Nausicaa Consulting works across four engagement structures depending on the service and project stage. Rates are not published — fee schedule provided on enquiry.

Publishing an unbenchmarked rate creates a ceiling that is very hard to move later. Instead, the table below describes how each engagement type is structured. Contact us to discuss your project and receive a fixed-fee proposal or indicative fee schedule.

Engagement model Applies to How it is priced
Fixed-fee package Market soundings, rate builds, WAIPS/APP participation plans, subcontractor pre-qualification. Defined scope, defined deliverable, defined turnaround. Fixed price agreed before work begins. Not billed hourly. Scope changes are quoted as variations.
Project-based contract administration Contractor-side CA or Superintendent's Representative for a defined project. Duration-based engagement from contract award through to close-out. Monthly fee agreed at project start, priced against contract duration and complexity. Not a day rate — incentive is resolution, not hours. Multiple concurrent projects supported.
Standing retainer Embedded commercial support for contractors with continuing Kimberley activity — ongoing claim management, subcontract oversight, market intelligence. Monthly retainer covering a defined scope of recurring work. Provides priority access and continuity across projects.
Day rate Site supervision, project administration, discrete short-form assignments, expert review. Day rate quoted per engagement. Minimum commitment applies. Client travel recovered as a disbursement.

Fee schedule on enquiry

Rates depend on project complexity, duration, contract value and role (contractor-side CA vs Superintendent's Representative). To receive a fee schedule or fixed-fee proposal for your project, contact us with a brief description of the works, the contract standard in use, and the project programme.

Client travel and accommodation is recovered as a disbursement at cost. GST applies to all fees. A written conflict check is conducted before any engagement is accepted.

What to include in your enquiry

The more context provided, the more accurate the proposal. Useful information includes:

  • Contract standard (AS4000, AS2124, bespoke)
  • Project location and site programme
  • Contract value and estimated duration
  • Number of subcontractors in scope
  • Role required (Superintendent's Rep, contractor-side CA, or other)
  • Project stage (pre-tender, tendering, awarded, in delivery)
