Engagement models.

Two service lines, two fee structures. The day rate is published. Project and retainer fees are quoted against the specific engagement, because an unbenchmarked published rate creates a ceiling that is hard to move later.

Published day rate

$1,280 per day

Based on $160 per hour over an eight-hour day. Applies to supervision, site attendance, project administration and discrete short-form assignments. Minimum commitment applies. Client travel and accommodation recovered as a disbursement at cost. GST applies.

Sell-side: the three-stage structure.

For Kimberley businesses engaging a Fractional Commercial Manager. The structure exists because the cost of commercial management belongs in the price of the job — not in the business owner's pocket.

Stage 1

Tender support

Pre-submission support against a defined deliverable list. Fixed fee with a capped number of hours, agreed in writing before work starts.

Funded by the client, at risk. Incurred before award — if the tender is unsuccessful there is no contract to cost it into, and nothing further is payable.
Default on award Stage 2

Ongoing commercial management

On award, the engagement continues as ongoing commercial management for the duration of the contract. Monthly fee, minimum term.

Funded through tender preliminaries. Costed into the tender price as a preliminaries item and funded by the head contractor through the rates.
Stage 3

Buy-out

Where the client elects not to proceed with Stage 2 after award, a one-off buy-out fee applies in its place.

Also funded through preliminaries. This is what declining the ongoing engagement costs — not a cheaper alternative to it.

Read the structure carefully. Stage 2 is the default outcome on award. Stage 3 is what a client pays to decline it. These are not presented as two options to choose between, because the whole arrangement only works if the ongoing engagement is the normal path.

What tender support includes

Defined by deliverable rather than activity, so there is no argument later about what was covered.

  • Pricing review and rate validation
  • Preliminaries and on-cost build
  • Subcontractor and supplier sourcing
  • Participation plan preparation (WAIPS / APP)
  • Commercial response and schedules
  • Departures schedule and qualifications
  • One review cycle of the completed submission

Addenda included up to an agreed number; beyond that a fee variation applies. Best-and-final-offer rounds and post-tender negotiation are not included and are quoted separately. One fee covers one tender for one project — not one client, and not a panel submission spanning multiple projects.

The hours cap, and how it is handled

Tender support carries a capped number of hours. Nausicaa notifies the client in writing when 80% of the cap has been consumed. Beyond the cap, work continues only where the client agrees in writing — either to a fee variation or to an hourly rate. No client receives an invoice for hours they did not approve.

What to include in your enquiry

The more context provided, the more accurate the proposal.

  • Which service line — delivering work in the Kimberley, or based here
  • Project stage — pre-tender, tendering, awarded, or in delivery
  • Contract standard, where one applies (AS4000, AS2124, bespoke)
  • Project location and programme
  • Contract value and estimated duration
  • Number of subcontractors in scope
  • Role required — Superintendent's Representative, contractor-side administration, fractional commercial management, or other
