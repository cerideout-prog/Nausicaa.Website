Before you rely on this

This list is indicative and not exhaustive. It is compiled manually from public sources and does not capture every opportunity in the region. Closing dates and requirements change, and addenda are issued that are not reflected here. Bidders must verify all details directly with the issuing body and register through the relevant portal to receive addenda. Nausicaa Consulting is not the issuer of any opportunity listed and accepts no responsibility for decisions made in reliance on this page.

Where this comes from

Sources and attribution.

Only public listings are included. Nothing behind a login or a paywall is reproduced, and every entry links back to its published source.

Compiled by the Kimberley Development Commission

The bulk of this table is drawn from Kimberley Weekly Tender Opportunities, compiled and published each week by the Kimberley Development Commission. The KDC does the work of finding these; we reproduce the title, issuing body, location and closing date, and link back to their published list.

If this is useful to you, subscribe to it directly. The KDC list is free, arrives weekly, and carries grants and business updates this table does not. We would rather you got it from the source — kdc.wa.gov.au.
