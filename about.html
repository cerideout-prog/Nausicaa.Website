The result is someone who can price a job, read a subcontract, understand a FIFO roster and a freight schedule, and have a straight conversation with a Broome business owner about how to improve their tender processes and win more work.

Why Broome

Based here, not visiting.

Broome is the base, not a service area. That matters more than it sounds. Being here means site attendance without a flight, claims answered in the same time zone as the works, supplier relationships built face to face over years rather than sourced from a register, and a working knowledge of who can actually perform in this market.

It also means being accountable in a small community. Consultants who fly in can afford to be wrong. People who live here can't.

The service area extends across the Kimberley — Broome, Derby, Fitzroy Crossing, Halls Creek, Kununurra and Wyndham — with work also undertaken in the Pilbara and Gascoyne.

Transparency

Acting for both sides of this market.

Nausicaa acts for contractors bringing work into the Kimberley, and for Kimberley businesses delivering it. That creates real potential for conflict, and sophisticated buyers look for it — its absence from a website reads as naivety rather than integrity.

The response is a documented framework rather than a promise. A small set of absolute prohibitions that apply regardless of client consent. A conflict check performed and recorded before every engagement. A withdrawal protocol decided in advance. Some engagements are declined, and the framework is published so that decision can be checked rather than taken on trust.

The limit that no clause fixes

Nausicaa is one person. Knowledge does not compartmentalise, and anyone reviewing this will know that. Where the same person cannot be separated from the knowledge, the engagement is declined rather than managed. A sole practitioner claiming an effective information barrier should not be believed — acknowledging the limit is the only honest position.
