Security of Payment

Fifteen business days. No extensions.

Under the Building and Construction Industry (Security of Payment) Act 2021 (WA), a party disputing a payment claim must issue a valid payment schedule within 15 business days. Miss it, and the full claimed amount becomes a statutory debt.

Administering Kimberley subcontracts from Adelaide or Perth means every claim, every variation and every notice runs on that clock — from 2,000 kilometres away. Nausicaa Consulting administers it here.
