The commercial layer

Estimate → Tender → Mobilise → Deliver → Claim & Vary → Bill & Close.

The same six stages sit behind every project. We can run all of them, or step in at the one that is hurting.

Stage 01

Estimate

Scope built and priced on real regional cost — mobilisation, seasonality, haulage and the availability of those actually positioned to deliver.

Stage 02

Tender

Bids written and priced to win without buying the job, behind a subcontract panel that holds through a wet season.

Stage 03

Mobilise

Head-contract scope unbundled into packages local businesses can actually deliver, with WAIPS, APP and IPP targets set against a real supplier base.

Stage 04

Deliver

Senior commercial oversight alongside your supervision: pricing, margin, forecasting and reporting you can rely on.

Stage 05

Claim & Vary

Claims certified or built, variations tested, alternate pricing options put on the table rather than left on it.

Stage 06

Bill & Close

Quote-to-cash through to final account, with acquittal reporting for grants that survives scrutiny.
