Did you know?

If you don't reply to a payment claim in time, you can end up owing the full amount.

If someone sends you a payment claim and you don't reply properly in time, you can end up owing the whole lot — even if you disagree with it.

The rules under WA's security of payment laws are strict, and the clock doesn't stop for site work, holidays or a busy week. Most businesses that get caught out weren't disputing the claim. They just missed the reply.

Getting this right is part of what a commercial manager does.

We act for both sides of this market. That is disclosed, not hidden.

Nausicaa acts for contractors bringing work into the Kimberley and for Kimberley businesses delivering it. In a market this size, a blanket prohibition on acting for both would be unworkable — so instead there is a documented regime a probity officer can inspect: a small set of absolute prohibitions that apply regardless of consent, a conflict check performed and recorded before every engagement, and a withdrawal protocol decided in advance.
