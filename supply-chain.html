Delivering in the Kimberley · Service

Verified supply chain and market sounding.

Not a directory. A maintained register where capability, tickets, insurances, plant, capacity and rate history have been confirmed first-hand — because a supplier list nobody has verified is worse than no list at all.

Scope of service

  • Pre-tender market soundings — trade category by trade category, from suppliers actually operating in the region
  • Kimberley and Pilbara rate builds — plant, labour, materials and subcontractor rates, benchmarked and documented
  • Supply constraint identification — lead times, seasonal shutdowns, capacity limits and programme risks
  • Subcontractor pre-qualification — panel establishment, documentation and maintenance
  • Supplier capability and financial assessment — can they perform, and can they carry the subcontract value
  • Aboriginal business identification — for WAIPS and APP purposes, with capacity confirmed
  • Invitation to tender coordination — packaging, invitation and response management
  • Post-award subcontract formation — procurement support through to execution

What "verified" means here

The distinction matters, because most supplier information available to a contractor pricing Kimberley work is self-reported and unchecked.

A directory tells you

  • The business exists
  • What it says it does
  • An ABN and a phone number
  • Whatever it entered on a registration form

The register records

  • Capability confirmed against actual work performed
  • Tickets, licences and competencies sighted
  • Insurances current and sighted
  • Plant held, and its condition and availability
  • Present capacity — what they can take on now
  • Rate history from Nausicaa's own market work

The cost of pricing Kimberley rates from Perth

  • Kimberley labour, plant and subcontractor rates carry a structural premium over Pilbara or Perth equivalents
  • Remoteness, wet-season programme disruption, a limited supplier base and real FIFO logistics costs all feed into it
  • The premium is not uniform across trade categories — plant-intensive work prices differently to trade labour, and FIFO subcontractors differently to Broome or Kununurra-based operators
  • A rate build that does not distinguish between those categories will be wrong in some of them, and wrong in the direction that costs the contractor

Market sounding is not a desktop exercise

It requires actual conversations with suppliers operating in the region — trade contractors, plant hire operators, materials suppliers, logistics providers — to establish what is available, at what lead time, and at what rate. That information is not on a website, and it changes.

The output is a rate build reflecting what the Kimberley market will actually price, a register of supply constraints that affect programme, and an assessment of which subcontractors have capacity for your project rather than which ones are already fully committed.

Pre-qualification as a commercial function

  • Frequently treated as administrative — collecting SWMS, insurance certificates and licences
  • Administered properly it establishes which subcontractors are actually available for your programme
  • Which hold the WHS management systems your head contract requires
  • And which carry the financial capacity for the subcontract value you need to place
  • In a market this size the pool is small and ranges from well-capitalised operators to sole traders carrying more risk than they can manage — a process that ignores financial capacity builds the wrong panel

Ownership of the Capability Register

The Capability Register, and all data, verification records and methodology within it, remain the exclusive property of Nausicaa Consulting. Deliverables may incorporate extracts, and clients receive a non-exclusive licence to use those extracts for the project for which they were provided. No licence is granted to reproduce, distribute or use the Register or its extracts for any other project or purpose.

No client confidential information is reproduced into the Register. Rate history held in it is sourced from Nausicaa's own market work, from published data, or with the supplier's written permission.

Conflict management

Nausicaa accepts no commission, referral fee, rebate or other benefit from any supplier it recommends, introduces or assesses — all income derives from disclosed client fees. Where a business named in a sourcing deliverable is a current or former retained client of Nausicaa, that is disclosed in the deliverable itself, not only in correspondence. Nausicaa will not act for more than one party on the same project where those parties are in a contractual relationship with one another.
