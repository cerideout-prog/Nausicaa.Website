## Positioning statement

Broome-based commercial and project-delivery working across the Kimberley. We run the
commercial layer of a project alongside your supervision — estimating, tendering, contract
administration, claims and variations, through to billing.

**Value chain:** Estimate → Tender → Mobilise → Deliver → Claim & Vary → Bill & Close

## WHO WE WORK WITH

**Entering the Kimberley** — "We can win the work. Who runs it up there?"
Regional cost priced honestly, a subcontract panel that holds through a wet season, and a
commercial lead in Broome.

**Kimberley Businesses** — "The commercial skills are the constraint."
Estimating discipline, claims and variations recovered, and useful reporting you can trust.

**Principals & Asset Owners** — "Who can see what a contractor is claiming?"
Superintendent's Representative and contract administration from someone who has won tenders,
reviewed contracts and built claims.

## WHAT WE DO

**Contract Administration & Superintendent's Rep**
Claims certified, variations tested, alternate pricing options, and acquittal reporting for
grants that survives scrutiny.

**Fractional Commercial Management**
Senior commercial oversight on a retainer: pricing, margin, forecasting and quote-to-cash.

**Tendering & Estimating**
Bids priced on real regional cost — mobilisation, seasonality, haulage, availability from those
positioned to deliver your project.

**Regional Mobilisation & Local Supply Chain**
Head-contract scope unbundled into packages local businesses can actually deliver. Including
advice on WAIPS, APP and IPP targets.
