Principals & Asset Owners

“Who can see what a contractor is claiming?”

Superintendent’s Representative and contract administration from someone who has won tenders, reviewed contracts and built claims — so the claim in front of you is read by someone who knows how it was assembled.

Nausicaa acts for principals and asset owners who need an independent read on what is being claimed against a contract — progress claims assessed on their merits, variations tested before they are priced in, and reporting that stands up when someone asks how a number was arrived at.

Superintendent’s Representative is offered as a service. Every engagement is subject to a conflict check performed and recorded before work starts.

Claims assessed on their merits

Progress claims read against the contract and the work actually in place, not against the last claim.

Variations tested before they land

Entitlement and pricing examined separately, so a variation is not conceded on presentation.

Security of payment timeframes held

The statutory clock does not stop for site work or a busy week. Responses go out inside time.

Reporting that survives scrutiny

Acquittal and progress reporting a probity officer or a funding body can follow.
